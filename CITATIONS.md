# Citations

Exact editions referred to in the code and in `THEORY.md`.

- Ashby, W. Ross. *An Introduction to Cybernetics*. London: Chapman & Hall, 1956. (Variety, p. 126; the Law of Requisite Variety, p. 207.)
- Beer, Stafford. *Brain of the Firm*. London: Allen Lane, 1972. Second edition, Chichester: Wiley, 1981, which adds the account of Chile.
- Beer, Stafford. *The Heart of Enterprise*. Chichester: Wiley, 1979. ("Variety absorbs variety", p. 286; the Principles of Organization.)
- Beer, Stafford. *Diagnosing the System for Organizations*. Chichester: Wiley, 1985. The applied, diagnostic companion to the two above.
- Medina, Eden. *Cybernetic Revolutionaries: Technology and Politics in Allende's Chile*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 2011. The standard scholarly account of Project Cybersyn.

Page references for the triple-vector definitions in *Brain of the Firm* are not given here because I have not re-verified the exact wording against the text; `Metrics` is labelled accordingly.
