what this can and cannot prove

It can demonstrate the internal logic of the VSM as Beer specified it; compute Ashby's variety and the requisite-variety inequality exactly over a finite state space you stipulate; show, by test, that an algedonic alarm propagates up the recursion exactly when the model's own rule says it should; and serve as an executable, cited glossary of cybernetic regulation.

It cannot prove any real organization is viable or predict its behaviour, validate the VSM as a theory of organizations, control any robot, or measure real-world variety. The algedonic rule — a threshold held for a timeout — is my operationalization; Beer described the channel and wrote no algorithm.
